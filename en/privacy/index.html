Privacy Policy

Jiaying accesses the family media servers you choose. Data minimization and local processing are core principles of the app.

Effective September 1, 2026

Summary

Jiaying does not track you, show ads, or collect personal data for the developer. Your media travels only between the device running Jiaying and the NAS, Family Media, or Jellyfin server you choose.

Data we do not collect

Jiaying contains no advertising SDK, behavioral analytics, cross-site tracker, or developer-operated data collection service. The developer does not receive your photos, videos, server addresses, account details, viewing history, or device identifiers.

This website uses no cookies, analytics services, third-party scripts, or forms.

Information stored on your device

  • Server addresses, source configuration, and playback preferences are stored in UserDefaults on the device.
  • Your Jellyfin username, user ID, and session token are stored in the system Keychain.
  • Your Jellyfin password is used only for the login request and is not persisted.
  • iPhone, iPad, and Apple TV keep separate settings. Jiaying does not sync this information between devices.

Network access and media transfer

Jiaying contacts Family Media or Jellyfin according to your configuration. Browsing requests, thumbnails, photos, videos, and playback status travel only between your device and selected server. The developer does not operate a relay server and cannot view that content.

On Apple devices, Jiaying requests Local Network access to discover or connect to servers on your home network. You control whether this permission is granted.

Sharing and retention

Because the developer does not collect personal data, that data is not sold or shared with third parties. Signing out of Jellyfin or removing its configuration clears the saved session. Apple platforms control how Keychain data is handled after an app is uninstalled.

Security guidance

Connect only to servers you trust. Do not expose a family media service without authentication directly to the public internet. For remote access, use a secure network solution you understand and trust.

Updates and contact

If Jiaying changes how it handles data, this page will be updated with a new effective date. Email support will open before the App Store launch. Until then, contact the developer through Jiaying Support Issues. Read the privacy notice on the support page before posting.
