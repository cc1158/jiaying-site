Your media stays on your network

Connect to your server
Jiaying does not provide a public media cloud. Photos and videos travel between your device and configured server.
No behavior tracking
The site and app do not use advertising, analytics services, or cross-site trackers.
Settings remain on device
Server addresses and preferences are stored locally. Jellyfin session credentials are stored in the system Keychain.

Designed for three screens

iPhone and iPad use touch navigation, while Apple TV uses remote focus navigation. Each device keeps its own settings without syncing server configuration.
